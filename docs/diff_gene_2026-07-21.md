## Refinements (2026-07-21b)
- **Serial methylation** at the diff locus: me1_diff → me2_diff → me3(Mk_diff), PRC2-written, FAST me1/me2, **SLOW
  me2→me3** (the de-novo lag) — so me3 is genuinely division-rate-sensitive.
- **High activation threshold** (K_diff=0.28): the gene is robustly repressed and fires only when me3 falls *below ~0.28*
  (from ~0.86 normal) — normal and moderately-fast cells stay poised.
- **Reframed as premature gene ACTIVATION** (readout = Diff), not full differentiation. Differentiation is complex and
  G0-dependent: downstream, a fired cell **completes its final mitosis then exits to G0** (a few residual divisions,
  then CyclinD1 repressed via `diff_exit`). The model does not attempt the detailed differentiation program.
- Result: normal me3≈0.86 / gene OFF / 27 divisions; sustained Tc≲12 h OR EZH2i → me3 collapses → gene fires → exit.

## What it is
A **passive differentiation-sentinel locus** — it reads the mark machinery but does **not** feed back to the cell cycle,
so the default model is unchanged. It is **bivalent / poised**:
- `Mk_diff` — H3K27me3, written by the **same EZH2/PRC2** and diluted by the **same S-phase replication** as CyclinD1.
- `H3K4_diff` — H3K4me3, a constant **poising** active mark (0.55).
- `Diff` — differentiation-gene activity; fires via `diff_drive = H3K4·K^n/(K^n + Mk_diff^n)` — i.e. **only when
  H3K27me3 dilutes below the poise** (switch-like, because the gene is already H3K4me3-loaded and "ready").

The writing is deliberately **slow** (restoration timescale ~ Tc — the me3 de-novo lag), so `Mk_diff` is
**division-rate-sensitive**: at normal Tc the writer keeps up; when the cell divides too fast, `ln2/Tc` outruns the
writer and the mark collapses.

## Results (fig panels)
- **A — normal GNP (Tc≈22 h):** `Mk_diff`≈0.89 (heavy, with S-phase dips), `Diff`≈0.05 → **poised, held OFF** (bivalent).
- **B — EZH2i pulse:** the writer is blocked → H3K27me3 collapses → the poised gene fires (`Diff`→1) = **premature
  differentiation**. (This matches the real GNP phenotype of EZH2 loss.)
- **C — divide too fast (Tc → 10 h):** `Mk_diff` falls 0.9→0.6, crosses the H3K4me3 poise, `Diff`→0.6 = **premature
  activation** — the **speed limit** of the framework.
- **D — EZH2i dose-response:** graded writer loss → switch-like differentiation (second route).

## Two routes to premature differentiation
1. **Divide too fast** — dilution outruns the slow writer (the speed limit). Requires Tc ≲ 11 h, near v44's ~9 h floor.
2. **Lose the writer** (EZH2i / EZH2 down) — more decisive, because the writer otherwise **robustly defends** the mark
   (the `(1−Mk)` substrate term speeds rewriting as the mark drops). This is the cleaner, biologically-grounded trigger.

## Coupled to cell-cycle EXIT (differentiation is now a real event)
Two feedbacks make `Diff` firing a genuine, irreversible exit:
1. **Diff represses CyclinD1** — `diff_exit := 1 − g_diff_exit·Diff⁴/(K_diff_exit⁴+Diff⁴)` (g=0.97, K=0.4) multiplies
   the CyclinD1 transcription (proneural TFs repress cyclins). A fired cell drops CyclinD1 below commitment → arrest.
2. **Bivalent resolution** — `Mk_diff_erase`: an *active* gene erases its own H3K27me3 (transcription-coupled KDM6B),
   a sharp Hill (k=0.12, K_erase=0.70, n=10) that **latches** `Diff` ON once it crosses ~0.7.

Net = a **bistable / hysteretic** differentiation switch:
- **Normal & mildly-fast cells stay poised and cycling** (Diff excursions ≤0.28; e.g. Tc≈19 h → still cycling).
- **A strong push commits**: sustained Tc ≲ 17 h (via μ) OR EZH2i → Diff latches to 1.0, CyclinD1 collapses, and
  **divisions flatline** (~8 residual, then post-mitotic). The figure shows cumulative divisions stopping (B, C) and
  the sharp commitment vs growth-drive μ (D).
- **Design caveat:** the erase threshold `K_erase` must sit *above* the normal Diff excursions — a lower value made
  even normal GNP differentiate (the positive feedback destabilizes the poised state).
